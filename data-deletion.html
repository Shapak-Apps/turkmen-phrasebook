Data Deletion — Ykjam Terjime

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary: Ykjam Terjime does not store any user data on servers. The only data is a few settings kept on your device. To delete them, uninstall the app or clear its storage.

What the app stores on your device

  • Interface language
  • Language of your conversation partner
  • App preferences
  • A note that the app has already been opened once

Data left by older versions of the app (for example, favorites from version 1) is also stored only on your device and is removed in the same way.

How to delete your data

Android

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Ykjam Terjime.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage.

Or uninstall the app.

iOS

Press and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App.

Requests

If you have a question about your data, write to shapak.apps@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. We hold no data about you, and we can confirm this in writing.
