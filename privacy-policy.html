Privacy Policy — Ykjam Terjime

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Seydi Charyyev

This policy covers the Ykjam Terjime mobile app for Android and iOS.

Summary

Ykjam Terjime does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no registration, no analytics and no advertising.

Data collection

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. We do not run any server that receives data from the app.

Data on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device, for example the interface language and the language of your conversation partner. These settings never leave the device. Deleting the app removes them. See Data deletion.

Permissions

On Android the app declares only the internet and network state permissions. The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or your files.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
